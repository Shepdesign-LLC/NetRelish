The first preview of the native NetRelish for Mac. It's the direct-download build, signed with our Developer ID and notarized by Apple. It needs macOS 27.

This is a preview, not 1.0. Things will change, and the Pantry it makes may not carry over to 1.0. 1.0 comes to the Mac App Store on Dec 14.

## What works

- **Bench:** tabs and an address bar on WebKit. ⌘T opens a new tab and ⌘W closes one. Links that open a new window or a popup open as a new tab in the same Jar.
- **Shelf:** the Jar rail. ⌘1–9 switches Jars.
- **Jars as browsing profiles:** each Jar has its own cookies, logins and storage.
- **Brine:** idle tabs sink after their Jar's shelf life, and Restore brings them back where you left them. Pinned tabs never sink.
- **Smart Jars:** Brine, Today, Sealed and Sinking soon fill themselves. ⌃⌘1–4.
- **Capture** with ⌘D, into Brine.
- **Seal** with ⌘S: preserve-and-close a page as a web archive, full-text indexed. A sealed page opens its saved copy, read only.
- **Reader view** with ⌘⇧R: the article alone, in clean type. Press it again for the page.
- **The Panel** with ⌘⌥I: the page in front of you, as your Pantry sees it. It shows its jar, where it came from, when it was captured and sealed, and its labels, which you can add and remove.
- **⌘K:** capture, seal, jump to a Jar, or find anything in your Pantry.
- **Fill a form** from your Me card with ⌘⇧F. The card stays in your Mac's Keychain.
- **Links for other apps:** mail and phone links open their app. Any other app link asks first.

## Not in this preview

- The Share extension, and the rest of Shortcuts.
- Built-in Recipes.
- Pro: reseal & diff and provenance export.

## Good to know

- **No updater.** This build doesn't check for updates and never will. New previews are posted here.
- **Your browsing never leaves your Mac.** No accounts, no analytics, no cloud sync.
- **Verify the download:** each disk image comes with its SHA-256 checksum.
