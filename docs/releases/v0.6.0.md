The fifth preview of the native NetRelish for Mac. Same as 0.5.0, plus a new look for the window, a board for each jar, a Seal you can watch, and the Zest tools built in. It's the direct-download build, signed with our Developer ID and notarized by Apple. It needs macOS 27.

This is a preview, not 1.0. Things will change, and the Pantry it makes may not carry over to 1.0. 1.0 comes to the Mac App Store on Dec 14.

## New in 0.6.0

- **A new look for the window.**
  - **Tabs join the page.** The front tab is white and attached to the page under it, with a lip on top in its jar's color. The tabs behind it are light gray.
  - **The Shelf is a rail of big jars.** Each jar is drawn in its own color, above its name. Today, Sealed and Sinking soon sit below the jars, and Zest sits at the bottom. ⌘1–9, ⌃⌘1–4, the right-click menus and dragging all work as before.
  - **Brine is a pill in the bottom bar.** It lights up only for tabs that sank since you last opened Brine.
  - **⌘K floats over the window.** What it finds is grouped: Pantry, Open tabs, Commands. Esc gives the keyboard back to the page.
  - **The Panel starts closed.** ⌘⌥I opens it. If you already chose open or closed, your choice is kept.
- **A board for each jar.** Click the NetRelish mark at the top left, or the jar's name beside it. The board shows the jar's open tabs and what it keeps. A page you sealed is a card with a **Sealed** pill. Cards take the keyboard, and Space or Return opens one.
- **A Seal you can watch.** Press ⌘S and a green band runs along the top of the window while the copy is saved. When it's written, a **Sealed** pill lands, the tab closes, and the jar fills in its own color. If the save fails, the tab stays open and the address field says so. With Reduce Motion on, Seal is a plain fade.
- **The Zest tools, built in.** **Window › Zest** opens Studio, Mail, MD and Image in a window of their own.
  - **They work with Wi-Fi off.** The Zest window can't reach the web at all. Its fonts and everything else it needs ship inside NetRelish.
  - **Files open and save the Mac way.** Choosing a file opens the usual open panel, and an export opens the usual save panel.
  - **File › Print…** prints the tool in front. Save as PDF works from there.
  - **A web link you click in a tool** opens as a page in your active jar, in the main window.
  - **Zest keeps its own storage,** apart from every jar.
  - **While Zest is in front**, Seal, Capture, Fill from Me and Reader are grayed out. Click back into a page and they return.
- **Help › Privacy Policy and Help › Acknowledgments.** The policy opens on netrelish.com. Acknowledgments lists the open-source code and fonts NetRelish includes.
- **A page can't use your camera or microphone.** A page that asks is told no, and your Mac is never asked on its behalf. Choosing this page by page comes later.
- **Your Mac asks before a page reaches your network.** Open a router or printer page in a tab and macOS asks first, and says why.

## Still in this preview

- **Right-click and drag:** jars, tabs and Brine rows have menus, and you can move them with the mouse.
- **A saved copy that has changed says so:** a sealed page's header says **Unchanged since sealed**, or says the copy has changed.
- **Seal & Summarize:** two or three sentences about every page you seal, written on your Mac. It needs Apple Intelligence on.
- **Reseal & diff (Pro):** seal a page again and see the two copies side by side.
- **Proof of what a page said (Pro):** a fingerprint, a signed timestamp, and **Export Proof…** in the Panel.
- **Shortcuts:** Seal in NetRelish, Capture in NetRelish, Open a Jar, and Search Your Pantry, in Shortcuts, Spotlight and Siri.
- **Capture from the Share menu:** Share → NetRelish puts a page in Brine.
- **Bench:** tabs and an address bar on WebKit. ⌘T opens a new tab and ⌘W closes one. Links that open a new window or a popup open as a new tab in the same Jar.
- **Jars as browsing profiles:** each Jar has its own cookies, logins and storage.
- **Brine:** idle tabs sink after their Jar's shelf life, and Restore brings them back where you left them. Pinned tabs never sink.
- **Smart Jars:** Brine, Today, Sealed and Sinking soon fill themselves. ⌃⌘1–4.
- **Capture** with ⌘D, into Brine.
- **Seal** with ⌘S: preserve-and-close a page as a web archive, full-text indexed. A sealed page opens its saved copy, read only.
- **Reader view** with ⌘⇧R: the article alone, in clean type. Press it again for the page.
- **The Panel** with ⌘⌥I: the page in front of you, as your Pantry sees it.
- **Fill a form** from your Me card with ⌘⇧F. The card stays in your Mac's Keychain.

## Not in this preview

- Buying a license key for this direct-download build. Until you can, Pro stays unlocked here.
- Ask the Pantry. It comes in 1.1.
- In Zest: drafting with Claude, and images from the web. A web image in Mail shows a placeholder here, and the email you export still has the real image.

## Good to know

- **Pro is unlocked in this build.** Everything Pro works with no purchase. That changes when license keys go on sale, and we'll say so in the notes first.
- **Signing in with a passkey doesn't work in a jar yet.** Use a password for now.
- **No updater.** This build doesn't check for updates and never will. New previews are posted here. To update, quit NetRelish and replace it in Applications; your Pantry stays.
- **Your browsing never leaves your Mac.** No accounts, no analytics, no cloud sync. 0.6.0 adds no new network use: the timestamp still sends a fingerprint and nothing else.
- **Verify the download:** each disk image comes with its SHA-256 checksum.
