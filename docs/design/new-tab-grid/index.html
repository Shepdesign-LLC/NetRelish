What the note is

  • One note per jar, and one per kept item — the Panel shows whichever it is describing.
  • Markdown, the ZestMail grammar exactly: **bold**, *italic*, `code`, [link](url), a blank line starts a paragraph. Nothing else, so one grammar covers the app and Zest MD.
  • Stored as plain text, so Send to Zest MD loses nothing.
  • It is the last section, and it grows to the bottom of the Panel. Empty, it shows one line of placeholder and nothing else — no toolbar, no formatting bar.
  • Writing Tools work in it for free, because it is a system text view (manifest §11 claim 4).

The cost, so it's a real decision

ADR 0012 proposed moving notes to 1.1 because they need a schema change. That change starts in /design/NetRelish.json in System Designer and regenerates Swift — the Panel section itself is the cheap half. Three weeks from Dec 14 that is worth saying out loud rather than drifting into.

Notes

Why there is no search field on it

Because there is nothing honest to put in one. The address row navigates by URL and has no search-engine fallback anywhere in the repo — type a phrase that isn't an address and it goes nowhere. The Pantry is searched with ⌘K, which already groups Pantry, Open tabs and Commands. A box in the middle of a new tab would promise a web search this browser does not do. So the Counter sends you to the two real doors instead, and the address row above it is still right there for an address.

Where it shows, and where it doesn't

  • ⌘T, or launching with no tabs → the Counter.
  • Clicking a jar, its chip, or the cog → that jar's board, exactly as today. The board is about one jar; the Counter is about what to do next. Keeping them apart is what stops the board turning into a dashboard.
  • The counts are read at the moment it draws. Nothing on it polls, animates or refreshes while you look at it.

What is on it, and why nothing else is

Every card is a command the app has today, checked against the source. Three things a grid like this would normally carry are deliberately missing:

  • Ask the Pantry — 1.1 (ADR 0012). It exists today only as a DEBUG Design Kit tile.
  • Run a Recipe — there is no Recipes list, no recorder, and no ⌘R anywhere in the source. Seal & Summarize runs implicitly on Seal; that is the whole of Recipes in 1.0, and a card promising more would be a lie.
  • Recently closed, or a history list — not a thing this app has, and history is a banned label besides.

Relish, counted

Four uses on the Counter, all on the list: the one filled button (§4 #1), the selected smart-jar row on the Shelf (§4 #3), the caret and focus ring (§4 #7), the cog (§4 #8), and the lit Brine pill in the bottom bar (§4 #13). The job cards, their icons, the group labels and the keycaps are all neutral. A jar's own color appears only on its drawn jar, the active tile's wash and edge, the front tab's lip, and a card's border under the pointer.

What signing this off changes

  • Manifest → v2.2, with a changelog line: the Counter joins §8 as a component; §8 Shelf takes jar 44 and tile 100 × 72; the Panel gains a Note section; the jar-size list in "The jar, drawn" becomes 14 · 18 · 22 · 44 · 46.
  • NRShell takes the two new measures and ShellMeasureTests pins them, as it does every other number.
  • Notes need the schema change in System Designer first — see the panel above.
Three things only you can answer.
1 · Is the Counter the right word, or is it just "a new tab"? It would join the locked vocabulary in CLAUDE.md, which is a bigger deal than a view name.
2 · Does the Counter replace the board on ⌘T, or sit above the board's tiles and cards on the same screen? Drawn here as a replacement.
3 · Notes in 1.0 or 1.1? The view is cheap; the schema change is not, and ADR 0012 currently says 1.1.
